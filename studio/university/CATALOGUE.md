# ComfyUniversity course catalogue

Every lesson has a CPU lab with stored output, a quiz, and linked primary sources. Rebuild with `python studio/university/build.py --labs`.

## Level 100: Foundations: what happens when you press Queue

- **CU-101** [What generative AI actually is](100/CU-101-what-is-generative-ai/lesson.md). Learn a distribution, then sample from it. Three models on one dataset show the difference between learning and memorising.
- **CU-102** [Anatomy of a ComfyUI graph](100/CU-102-anatomy-of-a-graph/lesson.md). A small engine that follows ComfyUI's rules shows which nodes run, which come from the cache, and why changing the checkpoint re-runs six nodes, not seven.
- **CU-103** [Pixels, tensors and shapes](100/CU-103-pixels-tensors-shapes/lesson.md). Why IMAGE is [B, H, W, C] and LATENT is [B, C, H/8, W/8], what the VAE nodes do to cross between them, and where truncating 8-bit levels bites.
- **CU-104** [Prompts become numbers: tokens and embeddings](100/CU-104-prompts-to-embeddings/lesson.md). Follow a prompt to the tensor the sampler gets: byte-pair splitting, an embedding table that does not know its neighbours, and causal attention that does.
- **CU-105** [Seeds, noise and determinism](100/CU-105-seeds-and-noise/lesson.md). A seed is the starting noise and nothing more. A toy sampler with an exact denoiser shows what that does and does not fix when you change steps, samplers or hardware.
- **CU-106** [Your first image: checkpoint, sampler, VAE](100/CU-106-first-image-walkthrough/lesson.md). Follow one prompt through checkpoint, text encoder, sampler and VAE: the shape of every hand-off, and the 20 sigma values that the sampler walks down, checked against the ComfyUI documentation. Needs CU-102.
- **CU-107** [Models on disk: safetensors and families](100/CU-107-models-on-disk/lesson.md). Build and damage a safetensors file by hand, watch a pickle run code, and see how ComfyUI tells SD 1.x from SDXL by reading three shapes from a header.
- **CU-109** [Resolution, aspect ratio and the pixel budget](100/CU-109-resolution-aspect-ratio-pixel-budget/lesson.md). From an aspect ratio and a pixel budget to a size the VAE accepts: the square-root formula, what ComfyUI's size nodes round to, and how fast attention and VAE costs grow. Needs CU-103.
- **CU-110** [Reading a workflow JSON like a map](100/CU-110-reading-workflow-json-like-a-map/lesson.md). The same seven-node graph in the editor's save format and the server's API format, read with one recipe: follow the wire back, check the wiring, and translate between the two. Needs CU-102.
- **CU-111** [Files and formats: PNG metadata, MP4, WAV, safetensors](100/CU-111-files-formats/lesson.md). Build a PNG that carries a workflow, a WAV file and an MP4-style box stream by hand, and see why a reader walks chunks and boxes, why a flipped bit is caught, and what dropping metadata really removes.

## Level 200: Mechanics: how the pieces really work

- **CU-201** [Latent space and the VAE](200/CU-201-latent-space-and-the-vae/lesson.md). Train a tiny VAE on a CPU, and see why the midpoint of two latents is a picture while the midpoint of two images is a ghost. Needs CU-101, CU-103.
- **CU-202** [Diffusion from first principles](200/CU-202-diffusion-from-first-principles/lesson.md). Destroy data with noise on purpose, learn to undo one step, and train a real model on a CPU. Needs CU-101, CU-105.
- **CU-203** [Score matching, Tweedie and the SDE view](200/CU-203-score-matching-and-sdes/lesson.md). Why predicting noise is the same as learning the gradient of the data density, proved numerically. Needs CU-202.
- **CU-204** [Flow matching and rectified flow](200/CU-204-flow-matching-and-rectified-flow/lesson.md). Each training pair flies straight. The field you actually integrate does not, and that is the whole subject. Needs CU-202, CU-203.
- **CU-205** [Samplers are ODE solvers](200/CU-205-samplers-as-ode-solvers/lesson.md). Euler, Heun and DPM++ 2M written in five lines each, then measured against the exact answer. Needs CU-203.
- **CU-206** [Sigma schedules: where the steps go](200/CU-206-sigma-schedules/lesson.md). The scheduler chooses which noise levels the solver visits. Same model, same steps, different error. Needs CU-205.
- **CU-207** [Ancestral and SDE samplers: adding noise back](200/CU-207-ancestral-and-sde-samplers/lesson.md). Why a sampler that keeps injecting noise never settles on one image, and when that helps. Needs CU-205.
- **CU-208** [Guidance: CFG and what it really does](200/CU-208-guidance-cfg/lesson.md). One line of arithmetic, and it does not sample the distribution people usually say it does. Needs CU-205.
- **CU-209** [Negative prompts and cfg 1.0](200/CU-209-negative-prompts/lesson.md). A negative prompt is not a subtraction of concepts. It replaces the unconditional branch, and at cfg 1 it does nothing at all. Needs CU-208.
- **CU-210** [Precision: fp32, fp16, bf16, fp8](200/CU-210-precision/lesson.md). Where rounding bites: small LoRA deltas vanish, sums stall, and fp16 overflows at 65,504. Needs CU-103.
- **CU-211** [Quantization: int8, FP8, 4-bit and GGUF](200/CU-211-quantization/lesson.md). Plant a few large weights and watch int8 with one scale fall apart, while a scale per 32 weights and FP8 shrug it off. Needs CU-210.
- **CU-212** [LoRA: low-rank adaptation](200/CU-212-lora-low-rank/lesson.md). Why two skinny matrices can change a giant one, when they cannot, and what rounding does to a small update. Needs CU-210.
- **CU-213** [Inpainting masks and edge blending](200/CU-213-inpainting-masks-edge-blending/lesson.md). The sampler sees your mask on a grid eight times coarser than the picture: where a 3-pixel line vanishes, what grow and feather really do, and how the per-step blend works. Needs CU-201, CU-214.
- **CU-214** [Conditioning: img2img, inpaint, ControlNet, IP-Adapter](200/CU-214-conditioning-controlnet-ip/lesson.md). Every way of telling the model more than a prompt, placed at the point where it enters: what denoise really sets, what a ControlNet window covers, and why zero is not the same as absent. Needs CU-106.
- **CU-215** [Video basics: frames, fps and temporal latents](200/CU-215-video-basics/lesson.md). Why five seconds at 24 fps comes back as 117 frames, how a video VAE counts, and how attention cost climbs with duration. Needs CU-201.
- **CU-216** [Audio and music basics: waveforms to spectrograms](200/CU-216-audio-basics/lesson.md). Why a 6 kHz tone sampled at 10 kHz is heard at 4 kHz, what a longer frame buys and costs, and how ComfyUI turns seconds into audio latents.
- **CU-217** [Upscaling and tiling math](200/CU-217-upscaling-tiling-math/lesson.md). How ComfyUI cuts an image into tiles, why a ramp hides the seam, and why tiled decoding saves memory but costs 3.77 times the work. Needs CU-201.
- **CU-218** [Seeds, batches and variations in video](200/CU-218-seeds-batches-variations-video/lesson.md). The seed fills one block of noise in memory order: why a different frame count is a different video, why batch items can be redone alone, and why a blended noise must be rescaled. Needs CU-105, CU-215.
- **CU-219** [Depth and edge preprocessors: Sobel by hand](200/CU-219-depth-edge-preprocessors-sobel-by/lesson.md). Compute Sobel by hand, see why Canny blurs first and what its thresholds are measured in, and learn how a depth map becomes a grey hint. Needs CU-103.
- **CU-220** [The execution model: what runs, and in what order](200/CU-220-execution-model/lesson.md). ComfyUI starts from the output nodes and works backwards. Build its scheduler in 60 lines and watch a lazy input change the order. Needs CU-102.
- **CU-221** [Caching: why changing the seed does not reload the model](200/CU-221-caching/lesson.md). Each node gets a key built from its whole ancestry. Change one input and only what lies downstream runs again. Needs CU-220.
- **CU-222** [Sub-graphs and node expansion](200/CU-222-subgraphs-and-expansion/lesson.md). A sub-graph is a front-end idea that the server never sees. Node expansion is a server idea that the front-end never sees. Needs CU-220.
- **CU-223** [App Mode: a graph with a form on top](200/CU-223-app-mode/lesson.md). App Mode shows chosen inputs and outputs of a workflow as a simple form. The graph underneath does not change. Needs CU-109, CU-220.
- **CU-224** [The API: queue, watch, fetch](200/CU-224-the-api/lesson.md). Everything the page does, a script can do: POST a prompt, read events from a websocket, fetch the files. Needs CU-109, CU-220.
- **CU-229** [Color spaces, gamma and image math](200/CU-229-color-and-image-math/lesson.md). Half black and half white is not 0.5 in light: what blending and shrinking do to sRGB codes, and why SaveImage truncates. Needs CU-103.
- **CU-501** [History 1: GANs, the adversarial game (2014)](200/CU-501-history-1-gans-adversarial-game-2/lesson.md). Two networks play a game whose exact answer is a density ratio and a value of minus log 4. Then a real GAN trained on a CPU shows how far training gets from the theory. Needs CU-101.
- **CU-502** [History 2: Variational autoencoders and the latent idea (2013)](200/CU-502-history-2-variational-autoencoder/lesson.md). The 2013 paper in three checks on a model with a known answer: the exact gap in the lower bound, a gradient estimator with far lower variance, and an encoder that learns the posterior. Needs CU-101.
- **CU-503** [History 3: Pixel by pixel, autoregressive image models](200/CU-503-history-3-autoregressive-image-mo/lesson.md). An image as a product of conditionals: check the chain rule exactly, watch a causal mask make the future invisible, and count what sampling one pixel at a time costs. Needs CU-101.
- **CU-505** [History 5: CLIP and contrastive learning](200/CU-505-history-5-clip-and-contrastive-le/lesson.md). CLIP's loss as a table of similarities: what chance scores, how the temperature caps confidence, why more captions help, and zero-shot use on pairs never seen in training. Needs CU-104.
- **CU-506** [History 6: Denoising diffusion arrives (DDPM, 2020)](200/CU-506-history-6-denoising-diffusion-arr/lesson.md). The DDPM paper checked against exact answers: the posterior of a forward step, the weights the simplified loss drops, and why the two reverse variances barely differ. Plus which one ComfyUI's ddpm sampler uses. Needs CU-202.
- **CU-507** [History 7: Latent diffusion and Stable Diffusion (2022)](200/CU-507-latent-diffusion-stable-diffusion/lesson.md). What a factor-8 latent grid does to the cost of a layer, counted by PyTorch's FLOP counter, what the paper measured end to end, and the price it admits. Plus the three parts of a checkpoint in ComfyUI. Needs CU-201.
- **CU-508** [History 8: Adapters era: LoRA and ControlNet](200/CU-508-history-8-adapters-era-lora-contr/lesson.md). What a rank can and cannot follow, measured against the best possible matrix, and how LoRA and ControlNet both start as the base model and then begin to learn. With the matching ComfyUI code. Needs CU-212.

- **CU-406** [Scaling: sharding, batching and serving](400/CU-406-scaling-and-serving/lesson.md). From one GPU to many, and what throughput really means. Needs CU-305.

- **CU-409** [Distillation math: teacher, student, consistency](400/CU-409-distillation-math-teacher-student/lesson.md). Learn how to compress a complex diffusion model into a fast, few-step generator by mastering the mathematical bridge between teacher guidance and student consis Needs CU-308.

- **CU-411** [Evaluating video: a flicker metric](400/CU-411-evaluating-video-flicker-metric/lesson.md). Learn to quantify visual stability by calculating the temporal variance of pixel intensities to detect flicker in generated video sequences. Needs CU-404.

- **CU-517** [History 17: Studios and agents: when models became pipelines](400/CU-517-studios-and-agents/lesson.md). Discover how ComfyUI transforms static model calls into dynamic, agent-driven pipelines that orchestrate complex creative workflows. Needs CU-405.
