## Kolors is its own family

Kolors (Kwai) has an SDXL-style UNet but a ChatGLM3 text encoder, and the studio records it as its own family, not under SDXL; do not assume SDXL LoRAs or ControlNets carry over. It rendered on 2026-11-02 on its own instance through kijai's KwaiKolorsWrapper, from a hardened copy with two transformers-5 fixes, 25 steps at CFG 5. A peach still life: 24.8 s, peak 12.7 GiB, seed 20261102. Faults: the peaches have no fuzz and read as nectarines, colour runs a little hot, the window frame bends slightly. [code: studio/family_templates/index.json] [run: studio/family_status.json]

Kolors needs a cuDNN fix on its instance only. The pack's diffusers import pulls in the system cuDNN beside torch's own, and every convolution then fails. The studio puts the venv's cuDNN first on that one instance's library path, so the other instances are untouched. [code: psn/instances.py:59-63]

Kolors licence: a custom model licence (the card is tagged Apache-2.0); over 100 million monthly users, or a cloud platform, needs a licence from Kuaishou. [code: studio/licences.py:64]
